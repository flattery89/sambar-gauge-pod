1992 Subaru Sambar · Build log

A smart gauge for a kei truck

One round 52 mm gauge that shows turn-by-turn directions, what's playing, and (coming soon) engine RPM. It runs off the phone's wireless CarPlay, with no screen added to the dash.

The goal

Turn-by-turn, Beeline style

A big arrow, the distance to the turn, and the street name. The ring fills as you close in and turns amber in the last 500 ft.

Working

Works with Waze, Apple Maps and Google Maps

Use whichever app you like. The gauge shows a directions screen only while an app is actually navigating.

Waze being refined

Music on the gauge

Album art fills the dial, with song, artist, time left, and play, pause and skip you can tap.

Working

Tachometer

The Sambar never had a tach. The plan is to read RPM from the engine's ignition signal, with a redline around 6,500.

After parts arrive

Sound through the truck radio

Music and spoken directions go from the gauge to the truck's radio over Bluetooth.

After parts arrive

iPhone and Android

Wireless CarPlay works today. The same adapter also does Android Auto, which still needs a test on a real Android phone.

iPhone done

How it works

Carlinkit CPC200-CCPAWireless CarPlay / Android Auto adapter. On the bench.
Raspberry PiRuns the gauge and the radio link. On order.
Round touchscreenSized to drop into the open 52 mm gauge pod.
EN07 engine658 cc supercharged 4-cyl. No OBD port, so RPM comes off the ignition.

What's working, captured from the bench

Swipe or tap to cycle screens. Dots along the bottom show what's available right now. Directions come up on their own when a route starts and go away when it ends.

The same card, live on the gauge

Apple Maps sends the gauge its directions as data. Waze sends none, so the gauge picks Waze's turn card out of the CarPlay picture as it plays. (Street names in these images are placeholders.)
